  • Grades 8 & 9:
    2x2, 3x3, Pyraminx, Megaminx, 4x4

Competing, Groups, and Advancement Rules

  • Group Structure: Participants will be divided into groups of 6 or 8 depending on the total number of participants.
  • Age Divisions: All participants are categorized into two age groups: Grades 6 & 7 and Grades 8 & 9.
  • Round Progression Rules:
    • Under 15 participants: Direct entry to the Final Round.
    • 15 – 40 participants: 1st Round held, top 10 qualify for the Final Round.
    • Over 40 participants: 3-stage progression — 1st Round top 40% advance to 2nd Round, then top 8 partcipants will progress to the knockout finals.

Equipment & Staffing

  • Equipment: Timers, mats, and cube covers provided by Deccan Cubing.
  • Judges & Scramblers: Volunteer students from higher grades.
  • Runners: Randomly selected participants working on a rotational basis.
  • Briefing: Participants will be instructed on all rules via a thorough briefing video.

Important Notice

  • Arrival: Participants must show up 30 minutes early to their events. Latecomers will be disqualified.
  • Refunds: Strict no refunds on registration fees.
  • On-spot Registration: On-spot registrations will not be accepted.
  • Rules: Participants must be familiar with the WCA regulations as just an extra precaution.
  • Hardware: Participants must bring their own cubes. if a cube is damaged, the participant must borrow a cube from another person.
